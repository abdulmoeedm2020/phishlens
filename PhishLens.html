        What PhishLens checks

        Each check adds points when it fires. The total is capped at 100. Under 15 reads as no red flags, 15 to 39 as suspicious, and 40 or more as likely phishing.

        CheckWhat it looks forMax pts

        Limits

        PhishLens scores the shape of a link. It does not load the page, look up domain age, or compare against a live blocklist, so a brand-new phishing domain with a clean-looking address can score low, and an odd but harmless link can score high.

        Treat the result as a second opinion. For a link that matters, also check it against a live reputation service and confirm with the sender through another channel.
